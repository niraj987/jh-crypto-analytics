📈 Long/Short-Term Trend Lines (SMA)

The 50-Day (Cyan) and 200-Day (Yellow) simple moving averages trace major support and resistance channels. Price closing above these levels marks long-term institutional strength.

🔥 Relative Strength Index (RSI)

Tracks structural buy/sell exhaustion. Readings under 30 (Green Zone) show oversold discount opportunities, while readings over 70 (Red Zone) show speculative hype exhaustion (high risk).

⚡ Momentum Intensity (MACD)

Moving Average Convergence Divergence. Emerald green bars signal accelerated buyer inflow and momentum strength. Hot crimson bars show aggressive seller pressure.

🌪️ Average Volatility Index (ATR)

Tracks historical pricing dispersion. Higher ratios highlight wide, turbulent intraday price swings (typical for highly volatile meme coins), while lower ratios signal calm consolidation.
